# CyberBattleSim chain concepts

This pack carries the portable RAES view of the selected public size-10 chain:
an attacker progresses through a generated chain while a source-internal
defender scans and reimages compromised systems. The published task owns the
episode, evaluator, reward, availability, and termination intent that does not
belong in SDL.

The red participant is the qualified upstream `CredentialCacheExploiter`. Its
native observations, credential cache, action coordinates, policy state, and
proposals remain inside the separately installed simulator and adapter driver.
Only admitted RAES action contracts, bounded observations, evaluator evidence,
derived measures, diagnostics, and artifact identities are portable.

A recorded seed binds the Gym environment and action space. Python-global and
NumPy-global streams remain unbound, so validation or a successful run does not
establish deterministic replay, state equivalence, outcome equivalence,
benchmark comparability, or scientific validity.
