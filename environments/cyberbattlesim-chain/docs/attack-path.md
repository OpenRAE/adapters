# CyberBattleSim chain participant walkthrough

1. The red participant begins from the public entry position in the generated
   chain and receives only its bounded participant observation.
2. The qualified credential-cache policy proposes an exploration or credential
   exploitation action inside the driver. Native coordinates remain private.
3. The adapter exposes only the proposal's semantic action contract for RAES
   admission. A rejected proposal cannot advance the simulator.
4. One admitted action causes at most one source transition. The source-internal
   defender acts within that transition and is not a selectable participant.
5. The evaluator projects bounded cumulative-reward and terminal evidence after
   the episode; participant and evaluator views remain separate.

The objective is the selected public ownership task. This narrative does not
publish credentials, hidden topology, action masks, native results, reward
components, or an assertion that the objective will be achieved in any run.
