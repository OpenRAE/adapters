# CAGE-2 researcher example concepts

This example snapshots the full fixed Scenario2 module: User, Enterprise, and
Operational networks; thirteen source hosts; qualified image-derived services
and vulnerability intent; credential-free synthetic accounts; distinct Blue,
Green, and Red agents; and operational-service objectives.

CybORG-specific images, sessions, observations, reward projection, source
defects, and disclosed losses remain owned by the adapter and its pinned source
ledger. The portable module does not expose native state or claim replay,
equivalence, replication success, or scientific validity.
